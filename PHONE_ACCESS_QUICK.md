## 🌐 ALTERNATIVE - Open in Phone Browser

### Get Your Computer IP

**Windows:**
```bash
ipconfig
```
Look for: `IPv4 Address: 192.168.x.x`

**Mac/Linux:**
```bash
ifconfig
```
Look for: `inet 192.168.x.x`

### On Phone Browser
Open: `http://192.168.1.100:19006`
(Replace with your actual IP)
